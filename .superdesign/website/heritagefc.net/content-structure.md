# Heritage Fellowship Church — Home

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Religious organization / local church
- **What they do:** Sunday worship services, faith formation, community outreach, and giving programs
- **Target audience:** Existing members, first-time visitors, seekers in Springfield, Ohio area
- **Page goal:** Drive attendance (in-person or online), prompt connection/next steps, and donations

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Large-format photograph of diverse congregation (people laughing, conversing, engaged) with lightly rounded corners, bleeding to edges. Overlaid: centered white h1 "KNOW. GROW. GO." in display type, white h2 "WELCOME TO HERITAGE FELLOWSHIP CHURCH" below. White button "CONNECT HERE!" positioned center-bottom of image. Near-black background bleeds left and right of photo. Image ratio 1.78:1.

### Header / Navigation
Static horizontal navigation: logo (white "HFC" lockup, left) + three primary nav links in white (Events, Next Steps, Give) center-aligned + hamburger toggle (right). All on near-black background. No sticky behavior on scroll.

### Welcome Section
Centered white space. Olive-gray paragraph block. Dark gray h4 "Welcome to Church" + h2 "We're Glad You're Here!" + supporting paragraph (mission statement: KNOW, GROW, GO framing). Two buttons below: dark "VISIT" + outlined "MORE ABOUT US".

### Image Gallery
Horizontal carousel of 6 community/worship photographs (thumbnail ratio 2.55:1, 1.50:1 varied). Each rounded corners. Visible scroll hints on right edge. No autoplay.

### Call-to-Action Section
Centered white space. Dark gray h4 "JOIN US" + h2 "You're Invited!" Two buttons: dark "GET DIRECTIONS" + outlined "JOIN US ONLINE".

### Service Info Cards ×2
**Purpose:** Display in-person and online service times side-by-side.
**Layout:** Two equal cards (light gray background, lightly rounded), each with clock icon + h3 "Service Times" / "Online Service" + paragraph copy (times listed). Cards sit in a 2-column container.

### Next Steps Blocks ×4
**Purpose:** Guide visitors toward deeper engagement (faith exploration, event discovery, ministry involvement, giving).
**Layout:** 4-column uniform grid. Each block: dark gray gradient background, lightly rounded, white h3 ("Take Your Next Step", "What's Happening", "Get Involved", "Make an Impact") + supporting paragraph + dark button with arrow (START HERE, SEE EVENTS, VIEW MINISTRIES, GIVE NOW). Grid collapses to 2 columns at medium breakpoint.

### Testimonial / Tagline
Centered block on gray background. White italic blockquote: "Teaching the liberating power of the Gospel every week."

### Footer ×3 Card Section
**Purpose:** Contact, location, giving entry points.
**Layout:** 3-column uniform grid (white cards, lightly rounded). Left: envelope icon + h6 "Contact Us" + phone and email links. Center: pin icon + h6 "Find Us" + address link. Right: document icon + h6 "Giving" + "Give online" link. Below: social links (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube) + copyright + "The Church Co" link.

**Notable patterns:** Near-black page background throughout; white surfaces occupy ~78% of layout, dark gray ~17%, gray ~5%. All interactive elements use dark or outlined styling. Lightly rounded corners on all containers. Images maintain consistent aspect ratios within galleries and hero. Focus-visible and reduced-motion preferences honored.
