# Cowplain Church

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Independent evangelical church, Portsmouth area
- **What they do:** Offer Sunday services, weekday groups, events, and community engagement for all ages
- **Target audience:** Existing congregants, visitors, families, seekers curious about Christianity
- **Page goal:** Welcome visitors, surface key service times/contact methods, drive signup to groups & events

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Center-aligned h1 "Welcome To Cowplain Church" + tagline "For All People | All Ages | Transforming Lives For Jesus" in white over full-width church building photograph (1:1 ratio). Photograph is daylit exterior at golden hour. No decorative elements or floating cards. Header bar (logo + 8 navigation links + blue "Contact Us" button) sits fixed / static at top in semi-transparent dark overlay. Hero text is italicized display serif with negative letter-spacing, positioned mid-frame vertically. Below hero, horizontal carousel of 6 rounded-corner thumbnail images (3.64:1 ratio) showing congregation moments—preachers, small groups, worship—bleeding off right edge, inviting scroll.

### Header / Navigation
Horizontal bar: Cowplain Church logo (wordmark + italic "Church") in white at left; 8 text links (Home, About Us, Sundays, Events, Adults, Children, Contact Us, Christmas Panto) in white, centered; blue pill button "Contact Us" at right. Static positioning (not sticky). No hamburger visible in primary view.

### Cinderella Panto
- **Purpose:** Announce December event; drive ticket sales.
- **Layout:** Left column: h2 + 4 paragraph blocks (description + date/pricing + CTA link). Right column: 1:1 square image of panto cast. White background. Rounded corners on image.

### No Pressures. No Expectations.
- **Purpose:** Set tone; reassure newcomers of inclusive environment.
- **Layout:** Centered h2 + 2 paragraph blocks. White background. No image.

### Explore What's On ×3 (Children's Groups, Current Sermon Series, 2 Services)
Three cards in a single row. Each card: thumbnail image (3.64:1) above h5 heading. Rounded corners. White background with subtle shadow. Uniform grid.

### Be Part Of Cowplain Church - You're Invited!
- **Purpose:** Encourage attendance; reduce friction to first visit.
- **Layout:** h2 + paragraph + 2 button links ("Get Directions" + "Watch Online"), both styled as blue pills. White background.

### Join Us During The Week! / What Are We About? / Our Most Recent Service / Get Stuck In To Church Life.
Four sections ×4: alternating image side (left→right→left→right). Each: h2 (right section: "Week" in blue italics on dark text) + 2–3 paragraph blocks + 1–2 link buttons (blue pills). Images are 1.50:1 feature ratio with rounded corners. White background. Converged as one entry: alternating 2-col layout, image side flips.

### Affiliated With The FIEC
- **Purpose:** Establish institutional credibility; explain church network.
- **Layout:** Centered h2 + paragraph (2-col text center, 1-col image right). FIEC logo image. Blue band accent bar above section. White background.

### Connect With Us
- **Purpose:** Drive social media follows.
- **Layout:** h2 + paragraph + 3 social media icon links (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) centered. White background.

### Footer Contact Cluster ×4 (Get In Touch!, Call Us!, Durley Avenue PO8 8XA, Give To Cowplain Church)
Four equal columns, red background, white text. Each: h6 + icon + link(s). Icon (envelope, phone, pin, donation card) in white at top. Text links in white. Rounded corners on cards.

### Footer Bottom (nav + copyright)
- one-line stub: horizontal link row (Home, About Us, Sundays, Events, Adults, Children, Contact Us) + copyright text, all white on red. Centered. Charity registration number included.

**Notable patterns:** Alternating image sides on 2-col sections (hero left → week-panto right → sermon left → stuck-in right); white backgrounds with red footer band as full-width base layer; rounded corners on all images and buttons; blue accent on headings (word-level: "Church," "Week," "Life," "FIEC," buttons); white text on red in footer; navigation link repeats at footer.
